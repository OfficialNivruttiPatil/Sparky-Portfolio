◈ CLARITY

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

My primary focus is high-CTR YouTube thumbnails, visual brand identities, social media campaigns, and marketing creatives. Everything is built bespoke with custom lighting and compositional psychology.
Standard thumbnails are typically delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Urgent rush turnarounds (same-day delivery) can be accommodated depending on current studio load and schedule.
For thumbnails: your raw camera photos (high res, good lighting), video title/premise, and any specific text or reference channels you like. For branding: brief details about your target audience and core values.
Every package comes with revision rounds. Minor adjustments (text tweaks, color grading, small position shifts) are handled smoothly to ensure the final output is sharp and ready to deploy.
Projects typically require a 50% upfront commitment and 50% upon final approval before delivery of un-watermarked high-res files. Monthly retainer packs for creators are billed at the beginning of each cycle.
